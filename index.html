Hi, I'm Ray. I'm currently on a journey to master two distinct but powerful fields: software development and motion design. On the educational front, I'm studying software development through BYU Pathway Connect, where I'm focusing on building a deep understanding of programming logic, system architecture, and how to solve complex technical problems from the ground up. Alongside my formal studies, I am completely self-taught in motion design and animation, specifically focusing my energy on Cavalry. What makes this combination so exciting for me is how the two disciplines cross over; Cavalry's procedural, node-based architecture means the logic I learn in programming directly feeds into how I animate and manipulate visuals. I love the constant shift between the structured, analytical mindset required for software engineering and the fluid, creative freedom of visual design. I am incredibly hyped to be in this growth phase, pushing my limits every day, and leveling up my skills so I can build things that are both technically sound and visually alive.

Lagos, Nigeria

Lagos is a city defined by an almost electric, relentless energy, where the sheer pace of daily life sets a rhythm unlike anywhere else. As West Africa’s economic powerhouse, it spans from the packed, bustling markets of the mainland to the sleek, quiet office complexes and coastal strips of the islands. It’s a place where massive scale meets hyper-local hustle, driven by a population that adapts, innovates, and builds constantly amidst the chaos of a sprawling metropolis.
